# Original game artwork

Every SVG in this directory is original artwork made for Spaghetti Rig. None of it is copied or traced from another project. The files are self-contained: no fonts, no text elements, no linked or embedded raster images, and no external references. They use the shared house palette: ink `#173e35`, mint, paper `#f4f4e8`, yellow `#f9db48`, and the code-window and boss colours.

## Backdrop

- `circuit.svg`: 1600 × 1000 view box, opaque. A pale mint printed circuit board seen in slight perspective, with chips, pads, traces, vias, mounting holes and silkscreen marks, plus a soft haze toward the top right. It was generated from a seeded script. Use it as the full-screen backdrop for the "Click to play" prelude and as the title-screen texture, under a semi-opaque mint wash.

## Tool cards (`tools/`)

Every tool card has a 400 × 300 view box and is opaque and full-bleed: a mint background with a sunburst, ready for the picture box of a card. The card frame and border belong to the game, not the art.

- `ci.svg`: a toy robot on a conveyor belt stamping green check marks onto pull-request sheets.
- `prettier.svg`: a giant yellow comb that turns tangled spaghetti lines into neat parallel lines.
- `lint.svg`: a lint roller rolling over a code window and catching purple "any" gremlins, while one gremlin escapes.
- `casts.svg`: a type-check scanner gate, an ID card stamped with a proof shield and check, and a broken "trust me" masquerade mask on the floor.
- `spacing.svg`: chunky yellow `{ }` bookends pushing apart a block of code that now has blank-line breathing room.
- `size.svg`: a tape measure, a very long code scroll, and scissors cutting it into neat checked pages.
- `magic.svg`: the number slip "86400000" (drawn as paths) dropping into a top hat, which produces a named label tag with a clock icon.
- `complexity.svg`: a wobbly pyramid of nested bracket blocks flattened into a low staircase, with a gauge reading green.
- `jscpd.svg`: a photocopier spitting out identical code blocks, with a magnifier showing "=" over the duplicates.
- `folders.svg`: an overstuffed, bursting folder next to a tidy stack of small folders with a check.
- `aliases.svg`: a confusing signpost of `../../` arrows over a winding maze path, beside a straight road with a big `@` sign.
- `knip.svg`: pruning shears snipping a dead branch off a code plant, a broken cycle arrow, and a broom sweeping dry leaves.
- `boundaries.svg`: a three-tier layer cake (UI / domain / data). A green arrow is allowed to point downward; a red arrow pointing upward hits a striped barrier.
- `generic.svg`: a gear, a wrench and a check badge. Use it as the fallback for future tools.

## Tier emblems (`tiers/`)

Every tier emblem has a 240 × 240 view box, a transparent background, and a round teal badge with a mint centre.

- `foundation.svg`: stacked foundation bricks.
- `shape.svg`: a cube, a pyramid and a sphere.
- `complexity.svg`: a rope knot being untied.
- `duplication.svg`: a rubber stamp with two identical imprints.
- `structure.svg`: a folder tree.
- `architecture.svg`: classical columns on a blueprint.
- `generic.svg`: a star. Use it as the fallback.

## Boss silhouettes (`bosses/`)

Every boss has a 240 × 240 view box and a transparent background. Each is a dark `#2f202a` silhouette with an orange `#ff6d21` rim and a blurred glow made with an SVG filter. Show them on locked tiers.

- `foundation.svg`: "The Friday PR", a horned window monster covered in +/− diff marks.
- `shape.svg`: "The 900-line file", a towering scroll.
- `complexity.svg`: "The if-pyramid".
- `duplication.svg`: "The clone army", five identical window monsters.
- `structure.svg`: "The tangle", a ball of cables with a face.
- `architecture.svg`: "The god module", a crowned central module wired to satellite modules.
- `generic.svg`: a single angry code window. Use it as the fallback.

## Title-screen decorations (`deco/`)

All decorations have transparent backgrounds.

- `burst-star.svg`: 120 × 120. A yellow five-point comic star with an ink outline and a highlight.
- `burst-spiky.svg`: 160 × 160. A spiky yellow comic explosion with a pale diamond highlight inside.
- `bracket-badge.svg`: 100 × 100. A tilted mint rounded-square badge with a `</>` glyph drawn as strokes.
- `sparkle.svg`: 60 × 60. A small four-point yellow sparkle.
- `spaghetti.svg`: 200 × 170. A cute tangle of spaghetti with eyes and a smile.
- `lightning-logo.svg`: 120 × 80. The game logo mark: ink curly braces `{ }` with a lightning bolt between them.
